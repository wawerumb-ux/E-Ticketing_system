About the system

Built for the ICT department.
Designed for everyone.

The ICT Support Portal streamlines how staff raise, track, and resolve technology issues. Whether it's a hardware fault, software request, or access problem — every ticket has a trail.

Ticket management

Raise a ticket in seconds. Set priority, attach files, and follow every status change from open to resolved — with a full comment thread.

Real-time tracking

Live status updates via server-sent events. Know the moment your ticket is assigned, updated, or resolved — no polling required.

SLA tracking

Response and resolution deadlines are tracked automatically. High-priority tickets surface first. Nothing falls through the cracks.

Knowledge base

Browse self-help articles before raising a ticket. The ICT team maintains a searchable library of common fixes and guides.

Role-based access

Staff raise and track their own tickets. Admins manage queues, assign technicians, and oversee all departments from one dashboard.

Works offline

The portal caches your recent tickets and loads instantly — even without a network connection. Writes queue locally and sync when you are back online.
