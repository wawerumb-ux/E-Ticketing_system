About

The ICT E-Ticketing sketchbook is the help desk looked at slowly — each plate is one stage of a ticket's life. Raise it on the staff portal, watch triage sort it by priority, see it land with a technician, track it against its SLA clock, follow the comment thread as it gets worked, then watch it close and walk into the audit ledger. Two portals and one engine: the staff surface stays plain while the admin surface opens the queues, and the core keeps working offline, queuing tickets until a connection returns. No tricks — everything here is the system itself, drawn in ink and a little colour.
